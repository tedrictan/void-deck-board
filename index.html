Why Void Deck Board?

The problem

  • Neighbours are close but disconnected. We live a lift ride apart, and many of us have never spoken.
  • Notices are scattered. Paper on lift lobby walls, a chat group per block, Facebook pages, CC posters. Most people see a fraction, and anyone outside the chat sees none.
  • Help and events miss the people who need them. Seniors, new residents and anyone not in the right group are left out, and organisers cannot reach everyone.

How it helps your estate

🤝 Ask for and offer help"Can someone help carry groceries?" reaches the people next door. That is gotong royong, made easy.
📅 Never miss what is onOfficial events show in a Happening soon strip, so more neighbours turn up and fewer weekends are spent alone.
🐱 Find lost things fastPets, keys and parcels are noticed by the people most likely to see them.
♻️ Share more, waste lessA free sofa or a spare fan finds a new home on the same block.
✅ Know what is officialOnly verified organisers carry the ✓ banner, so you can tell a real CC or RC notice at a glance.
🔓 Easy for everyoneNo account to read or post, big readable notes, and pinned to your postal code.
